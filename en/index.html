Photography is writing with light.

I look for the light that is born from darkness: the flash when everything goes dark, or the last light of the day as the sun goes down.

I'm Adur, from Eibar, a town of gunsmiths and bicycles, and I come from engineering, where nothing is left to chance. That's why every session starts long before the first shot: I scout the place, sketch the lighting, plan the poses and keep a plan B in case it rains. Before we meet you'll get a dossier with the idea, references and everything you need to know.

Sport has been part of my life since I was a kid. I know when the jump is coming, when the effort shows, when a dancer is about to hang in the air. That closeness lets me see events from the inside: not just what happens, but what it feels like.

For years I ran after-sales service teams and learned that the job isn't done at delivery, but when you recognise yourself in your photos.

We all have a light of our own. My job is to find it.

Frequently asked questions

You don't need to. I direct you throughout the session, and beforehand you get a dossier with the ideas and poses.
